DI:
inject a dependency to a class which needs it. The class maintains a local copy of the 
dependent object and updates it with the injected value.

DIP: (inversion)
is just creating abstract classes and minimal classes 

A combination of both is used in real systems .
EX:
Client needs a paymentprovider, so its inject by IPayprovider.
IPayprovider can be paypal,stripe,google
